Privacy Policy

Your camera stays useful without cloud AI.

Focra requires no registration. Local camera tools, pseudonymous service accounts, subscriptions and optional cloud analysis handle data as described below. A random account ID does not make account-linked data fully anonymous.

Effective October 13, 2026

Cloud image analysis is optional. You can decline it and continue using the camera, local composition tools, pose guides, filters and photo saving.

1. Scope and controller

This policy explains how Focra processes personal information when you use the mobile app, anonymous service sessions, App Store purchase features, cloud AI features and these legal pages. The app developer is the data controller for the processing described here.

The official privacy and support contact is support@focra.app.

2. Data processed on your device

Camera permission enables the live viewfinder. Pose keypoint processing, device attitude, local composition measurements and filter previews run on the device. Structured hand or head movements are not uploaded as a separate data stream.

  • Photos are saved to the system photo library when you choose to save them.
  • Photo-library access displays images you choose to view; Focra does not automatically upload your photo library.
  • Local preferences and cloud AI consent choices are stored on your device.

Cloud AI can additionally receive one viewfinder image and selected derived scene measurements with your permission, as explained in section 4.

3. Service accounts and purchase identifiers

Focra does not require registration or an email address for sign-in. Supabase creates a persistent pseudonymous service account ID and issues session tokens stored on your device. The account ID exists on the service as well as your device; it is not just a local credential.

The same account ID is used as the RevenueCat customer identifier to associate App Store transactions and Pro entitlements. Focra and RevenueCat use these records to grant, restore, revoke or expire access and provide subscription history and analytics. These identifiers and purchase records are account-linked even though Focra does not ask for your name or sign-in email. Apple handles payment; Focra and RevenueCat do not receive your full payment-card details.

4. Optional cloud AI

Cloud AI runs after you choose the feature and grant upload permission. You can revoke this permission in Settings > Privacy Policy & AI Data.

AI Composition

A request sends one compressed 288 × 384 viewfinder image, the analysis mode, language and basic scene/camera context, which can include frame size, zoom, subject box, composition anchors, brightness and horizon measurements. This enables composition, crop, zoom and filter guidance. It does not upload your whole photo library.

The request is authenticated with your service account and passes through the Focra server to the paid Google Gemini API. The account token is used by Focra and Supabase, not included in the Gemini prompt. Focra does not automatically remove faces or other identifying details from the image. Images, surroundings and request context are therefore not represented as fully anonymous.

5. How information is used

  • Authenticate and protect service access.
  • Confirm, restore and manage Pro entitlements.
  • Provide subscription history and operational subscription analytics through RevenueCat.
  • Generate the composition guidance you request.
  • Monitor request performance, troubleshoot failures and protect the service.
  • Enforce request limits and prevent automated abuse.
  • Comply with applicable law and respond to valid legal requests.

We do not use this data for advertising or cross-app advertising tracking, and we do not sell it to data brokers. An association with a service account is not the same as advertising tracking.

6. Retention and provider processing

Images and AI requests

The Focra server processes uploaded images in memory, excludes image bytes from its application logs, does not write its image copy to disk and discards that copy after responding. This does not mean every provider immediately discards the request. Under the paid Gemini API terms, Google does not use prompts or responses to improve its products, but logs them for a limited period for service safety and required legal or regulatory purposes. Google's terms, rather than Focra's own in-memory lifetime, govern that provider's retention.

Operational and infrastructure logs

Focra application logs record limited request metadata such as time, mode, size, status, duration, token counts, scores and error codes. Uploaded image bytes, full scene descriptions and raw account tokens are excluded from those application logs. Google Cloud Run infrastructure access logs can separately include IP addresses, request URLs, user-agent/device context, sizes, status and latency. Supabase processes account-linked authentication and security information. Such technical records can be associated with an account, device or request; they are not described as fully anonymous.

As of this policy date, the Focra Cloud Run default log bucket retains application and access logs for 30 days. Certain infrastructure audit logs have separate retention requirements. Google Gemini, Supabase and RevenueCat retain records under their applicable service terms, security needs and legal obligations; the 30-day setting is not a promise that all provider data has the same retention period.

Service and purchase records

Service account and entitlement records are retained as needed for access, purchase restoration, security, legal compliance and dispute handling. Uninstalling the app or revoking future AI uploads does not automatically delete remote records. Contact us to request deletion; legally required records may need to be retained.

7. Service providers and transfers

  • Supabase: service account authentication, sessions, entitlement records and security.
  • RevenueCat: purchase and subscription entitlement management, customer history and subscription analytics.
  • Google Gemini API: paid visual AI analysis and provider safety processing, under its service terms.
  • Google Cloud Run and Cloud Logging: Focra API hosting, access logs and operational diagnostics.
  • GitHub Pages: public legal pages; GitHub may receive ordinary website request information when you visit them.
  • Apple: App Store distribution and payment processing.

Providers may process data in countries other than your own. Their applicable terms and privacy practices govern their processing. Focra's service-account credentials are not deliberately supplied to Gemini as image-analysis prompt content.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Use Free camera features without registration.
  • Decline or revoke cloud AI uploads without losing local camera functions; revocation stops future uploads, not processing that has already occurred.
  • Restore purchases or manage and cancel an Apple subscription through the App Store.
  • Email support to request access, correction or deletion of service records. We may need information to verify which service or purchase records belong to you; do not send payment-card details.
  • Complain to your local privacy regulator where applicable.

If you email support, we process the email address, message and attachments you choose to provide to answer your request. This is separate from sign-in and is not used for marketing. Deleting service records does not itself cancel an Apple subscription.

Only upload images you are authorized to share. Images can reveal faces, private surroundings or other personal details; consider the privacy of others and obtain permission where required.

9. Children

Focra is not directed to children under 13, or the higher minimum age required in their country. We do not knowingly request account contact details from a child below the applicable age. Contact us if you believe this has occurred.

10. Security

We use encrypted network transport for production services, short-lived user sessions, row-level database policies, authenticated AI requests, rate limits, restricted server secrets and log redaction. No service can guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes and contact

Material changes will be published here with a revised effective date. If a change materially affects service-session or image processing, we will provide an appropriate in-app notice or renewed consent.

Privacy, support and deletion requests should be sent to support@focra.app.

隐私政策

不使用云端 AI，相机依然完整可用。

Focra 无需注册。本地相机功能、假名服务账户、订阅和可选云端分析按下文所述处理数据。随机账户 ID 并不意味着与账户关联的数据完全匿名。

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 13 日

云端图像分析完全可选。你可以拒绝，并继续使用相机、本地构图、姿势引导、滤镜和照片保存。

1. 适用范围与数据控制者

本政策说明你使用 Focra 移动应用、匿名服务会话、App Store 购买功能、云端 AI 功能及本法律信息网站时，我们如何处理个人信息。应用开发者是本政策所述处理活动的数据控制者。

正式隐私与支持邮箱为 support@focra.app。

2. 在设备端处理的数据

相机权限用于实时取景。姿势关键点处理、设备姿态、本地构图计算和滤镜预览在设备端运行，不会另行上传结构化的手部或头部动作数据。

  • 你选择保存时，照片写入系统图库。
  • 图库权限用于显示你选择查看的图片；Focra 不会自动上传整个图库。
  • 本地偏好和云端 AI 授权选择保存在设备上。

获得授权后，云端 AI 还可以接收一张取景图像及部分由本地计算得到的场景指标，详见第 4 节。

3. 服务账户与购买标识符

Focra 无需注册，也不为登录收集邮箱。Supabase 会创建持续使用的假名服务账户 ID，并签发保存在设备上的会话令牌。账户 ID 同时存在于服务端和设备上，并非仅在本地使用的凭据。

同一个账户 ID 用作 RevenueCat 客户标识符，将 App Store 交易和 Pro 权益关联起来。Focra 与 RevenueCat 使用这些记录授予、恢复、撤销或终止权益，并提供订阅历史和统计。即使我们不要求提供姓名或登录邮箱，这些标识符及购买记录仍关联服务账户。Apple 处理付款；Focra 与 RevenueCat 不会收到完整支付卡信息。

4. 可选的云端 AI

你主动选择功能并授权上传后，云端 AI 才会运行。授权可在“设置 > 隐私政策与 AI 数据”中撤回。

AI 构图

一次请求发送一张压缩后的 288 × 384 取景图像、分析模式、语言及少量场景／拍摄上下文，其中可能包括画面尺寸、变焦、主体框、构图锚点、亮度和水平线指标，用于提供构图、裁切、变焦及滤镜建议。不会上传整个图库。

请求通过你的服务账户验证，经 Focra 服务发送至付费 Google Gemini API。账户令牌由 Focra 和 Supabase 用于身份验证，不作为提示词传给 Gemini。Focra 不会自动移除图像中的人脸或其他可识别信息，因此不会将图像、周围环境及请求上下文宣称为完全匿名的数据。

5. 信息用途

  • 验证并保护服务访问。
  • 确认、恢复和管理 Pro 权益。
  • 通过 RevenueCat 提供订阅历史及运行所需的订阅统计。
  • 生成你请求的构图建议。
  • 监测请求性能、排查故障并保护服务。
  • 执行请求限制并防止自动化滥用。
  • 遵守适用法律及响应有效法律要求。

我们不将这些数据用于广告或跨应用广告追踪，也不向数据经纪商出售数据。与服务账户关联不等于用于广告追踪。

6. 保存期限与提供商处理

图像与 AI 请求

Focra 服务在内存中处理上传图像，不在应用日志中记录图像字节，不将自己的图像副本写入磁盘，并在响应后丢弃副本。这不意味着所有提供商都会立即删除请求。根据 Gemini 付费服务条款，Google 不使用输入或输出改进其产品，但会为服务安全及必要的法律或监管要求短期记录这些内容。提供商的保存期限由其条款约定，并非 Focra 自身内存副本的存续时间。

运行与基础设施日志

Focra 应用日志记录有限的请求元数据，例如时间、模式、体积、状态、耗时、令牌用量、评分和错误码，不包含上传图像字节、完整场景描述或原始账户令牌。Google Cloud Run 基础设施访问日志另行记录的内容可能包括 IP 地址、请求网址、用户代理／设备上下文、体积、状态和耗时。Supabase 会处理与账户关联的身份验证及安全信息。这些技术记录可能关联账户、设备或请求，不会被宣称为完全匿名。

截至本政策日期，Focra Cloud Run 默认日志桶将应用及访问日志保留 30 天；部分基础设施审计日志另有保存要求。Google Gemini、Supabase 和 RevenueCat 按各自适用条款、安全需要及法律义务保留记录。30 天的设置并不代表所有提供商数据均具有相同保存期限。

服务与购买记录

服务账户和权益记录按访问、恢复购买、安全、法律合规及争议处理的需要保留。卸载 App 或撤回未来上传授权不会自动删除远端记录。你可以联系支持请求删除；依法必须保留的记录可能需要继续保存。
