Focra support

Let’s get your camera working.

Find quick answers for camera access, Cloud AI, photo review and App Store purchases. If the issue remains, send us a short description and we’ll help.

Email supportWe aim to reply within two business days.

01

Camera or Photos permission

Open iPhone Settings › Apps › Focra. Allow Camera access and choose the Photos access level you prefer, then return to Focra.

02

Cloud AI is unavailable

Confirm you are online, reopen Focra and try once more. Cloud analysis may be temporarily unavailable; local camera tools continue to work.

03

Restore Focra Pro

Open Focra Settings, select Focra Free or Focra Pro, then choose Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID that made the purchase.

04

Photo review or export

Check Photos permission and available device storage. Keep the original photo until the saved or reviewed result appears in your library.

Focra 支持

让相机尽快恢复正常。

这里提供相机权限、云端 AI、照片点评和 App Store 购买的常见解决方法。如问题仍未解决，请把简短说明发给我们。

联系支持我们会尽量在两个工作日内回复。

01

相机或照片权限

打开 iPhone“设置”›“App”›“Focra”，允许相机访问并选择你需要的照片访问范围，然后返回 Focra。

02

云端 AI 暂不可用

确认网络连接正常，重新打开 Focra 后再试一次。云端分析可能暂时不可用，本地相机工具仍可继续使用。

03

恢复 Focra Pro

打开 Focra 设置，进入 Focra 免费版或 Focra Pro 页面并选择“恢复购买”。请使用最初购买时的同一个 Apple ID。

04

照片点评或导出

检查照片权限和设备剩余空间。在点评结果或保存结果出现在图库之前，请保留原始照片。
